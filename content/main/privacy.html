Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This site has no accounts, analytics or advertising trackers, and its fonts and images are hosted with the site itself. It only asks for personal information if you buy a ticket or pay a project deposit.

Tickets and Deposits

When you buy a ticket or pay a deposit, Stripe collects your payment details and passes us your name, email address and the order. We keep that to give you your tickets, run the event door, send your confirmation and receipt, and keep our financial records. We do not store your card number. We send your confirmation email through Resend and store orders in a database hosted by Turso. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

Like any website, the host (Vercel) may log standard request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site. This site cannot see or use those logs.

Links on this site lead to other services, such as Instagram, Spotify, Patreon, PayPal, and ticketing and scheduling pages. Those services collect information under their own privacy policies.

If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.
