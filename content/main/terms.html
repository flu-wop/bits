Tickets

Tickets are sold through this site and paid by card through Stripe. Each ticket has a unique QR code that is emailed to you and works once at the door. Keep it private: whoever scans it first is admitted. Refund and transfer rules for an event are shown on that event’s page and in your confirmation email. If an event is cancelled by BITS, we will refund your ticket.

Project Deposits

Hiring BITS for a project starts with a deposit. Deposits are non-refundable once work begins and are fully applied to your package. A deposit is payment for client work. It is never a donation. Package prices are starting ranges, and final quotes are custom.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe. BITS never sees or stores your card number. Prices are in US dollars.
