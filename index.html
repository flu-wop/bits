About Us

Breaks In The Simulation exists at the intersection of artistry and wellness. We are a creative collective and support system dedicated to the holistic development of artists in the music industry.

Who We Are

We design and deliver artist-centered experiences that recognize the full humanity of creative professionals. In an industry that often prioritizes output over wellbeing, we create spaces where artists can thrive—not just survive. Our work integrates creative development with social-emotional support, understanding that sustainable artistry requires both craft and care.

Our Founder

Founded by Rocky, a cultural worker and advocate deeply rooted in the music community, Breaks In The Simulation emerged from a simple truth: artists deserve comprehensive support that honors their creative vision and mental health equally. Rocky's background spans artist development, event curation, and mental health advocacy, bringing together these worlds to create transformative programming that meets artists where they are.

Mission

To cultivate sustainable creative practices by providing artists with the resources, support, and community they need to flourish personally and professionally.

Vision

A music industry where artist wellness is foundational, not optional—where creative brilliance and emotional wellbeing coexist, and where every artist has access to the support systems they deserve.

Values

Holistic support. Authentic community. Intentional care. Cultural sensitivity. Transformative impact. We believe in meeting artists with compassion, creating brave spaces for vulnerability, and building sustainable pathways for creative longevity.

Our impact extends beyond individual artists—we're cultivating a cultural shift toward wellness-centered creative industries, one conversation, one event, one artist at a time.

Wellness Support

Mental health care in the music industry isn't just needed—it's urgent.

Artists face unique pressures: the precarity of creative work, the vulnerability of public performance, the isolation of the creative process, and the systemic inequities embedded in the industry. Add to this the cultural stigma around mental health, particularly in communities of color, and you have a crisis that demands both immediate support and long-term systemic change.

We believe mental health support should be as accessible as streaming services. Our wellness programming provides education, resources, and community for artists at every stage of their journey.

Rewire with Rocky

One-on-one and small group sessions focused on rewiring unhealthy patterns and building sustainable creative practices. These sessions blend coaching, resource sharing, and compassionate accountability to help artists develop the emotional tools they need to thrive.

Workshop Series

Educational workshops covering topics like managing creative anxiety, setting boundaries, navigating rejection, building financial wellness, and more. Led by mental health professionals and industry experts, these sessions provide practical tools and create space for honest conversation.

Resource Referral

We maintain a curated network of mental health professionals, therapists, and practitioners who understand the unique needs of artists. Whether you need sliding-scale therapy, crisis support, or specialized care, we can help connect you to culturally-competent resources.

Community Networking Directory

A growing directory of artist-friendly services and resources—from mental health professionals to financial advisors, from creative spaces to community organizations. Building the ecosystem artists need to flourish.

If you're in crisis: Please reach out to the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline at 988, or text "HELLO" to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line. You are not alone.
