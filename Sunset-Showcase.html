Sunset Showcase

September 22nd, 2024 / 3-8pm / Margaret Place Hotel, New Orleans

This healing + arts experience combines elements from live concerts, health and wellness workshops, and immersive art exhibits for an experience that is both fun and entertaining while deeply impactful. Attendees will explore creative wellness and social-emotional intelligence through curated interactive design. The showcase features a powerful lineup of live music performances, healing arts practitioners and vendors, community health and wellness resources, and more! Explore what wellness looks like for you in an intentional, creatively engaging environment where you're sure to experience deeper connection to community, self, and art.
